How automated futures trading works here

There is no account to open with us and no money to deposit anywhere. The bot is a desktop application that talks to your exchange through an API key you create yourself.

1

Create a trading API key

On Binance or Bitget, generate an API key with trading enabled and withdrawals disabled. That single setting makes it structurally impossible for any software to move funds out of your account.

2

Run it in shadow mode first

Shadow mode trades a simulated balance against live market data. You can watch how the strategies behave on real conditions for a few days before committing any capital.

3

Go live on your own terms

Choose which symbols to trade from 500+ USDⓈ-M pairs, set your position sizing, and let it run. Trade notifications can be delivered to Telegram.

Why self-hosted matters

Most "trading bot" services ask you to deposit funds, or route your orders through their servers. This one does neither — and that difference is the entire security model.

Funds stay on your exchange

The software never has custody. Your balance sits in your own Binance or Bitget account the whole time, and you can revoke the API key from the exchange at any moment.

Orders execute from your PC

Strategy calculation and order placement both happen locally on Windows or macOS. Nothing is relayed through a third-party server, so there is no operator who can trade on your behalf.

No signal fees, no subscription

The bot itself is free to download and use. There is no paid signal group, no copy-trading arrangement, and no performance fee.

Frequently asked questions

Is this crypto trading bot really free?

Yes — the application is free to download and run, and there is no subscription or signal fee. You still pay your exchange's normal trading fees, which are charged by Binance or Bitget, not by us.

Which exchanges are supported?

Binance and Bitget USDⓈ-M futures are live today. Bybit and OKX support is in development. Each exchange has its own version of the app — choose yours from the cards above.

Can the bot withdraw my funds?

No. You create the API key yourself and leave withdrawal permission switched off. With that permission disabled, the exchange will reject any withdrawal request regardless of what software is connected.

Do I need to keep my computer running?

Yes. Because everything executes locally rather than on a hosted server, the bot only trades while the application is open and your machine is powered on and online.

Can I test it without risking money?

Yes. Shadow mode runs the strategies against live market data using a simulated balance, so you can evaluate behaviour before funding anything. We recommend starting there.

Can I lose money using an automated trading bot?

Yes, and you should assume it is possible. Crypto futures use leverage, which means a position can be liquidated and your capital can be lost entirely. Automation removes emotion from execution; it does not remove market risk, and no past result predicts a future one.
