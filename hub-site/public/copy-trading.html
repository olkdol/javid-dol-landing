Copy trading

The copy-trading profile is a different account from the live Binance account shown on this site. The live figures on this site are not copy-trading results.

The developer is the leader of these profiles and receives the profit share the exchange sets from followers' profits.

On a computer: open the link and continue in the exchange's app or site. On a phone: the link opens the exchange app if it is installed.

How copy trading differs from running the program

  • Your result is not the leader's result: entries are copied with a delay, at your size, and with your fees.
  • Exchanges usually take a profit share from followers for the leader; check the terms on the profile page.
  • Copy-trading rules and limits are set by the exchange, not by this program.
  • Leveraged copy trading can also lose your entire capital.
