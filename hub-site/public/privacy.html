Privacy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

The program

The program runs on your computer. Your API key and settings are stored there. The program does not send your key, balance or trades to the developer.

Downloads

When you download a build, the download server on that exchange's subdomain (bg., bn. or bb.javidtrading.com) records one row: the time, the platform (Windows or macOS), the country reported by the network, the referring site's host name, UTM campaign tags, the page you came from on this site, a coarse client type (browser, bot, script) and the browser's Sec-Fetch-Site value. Your IP address and full user agent are not stored. We use this to count downloads and see which channels work.

Browser storage

This site keeps where you arrived from in your browser's session storage (cleared when the tab closes) so a download can be attributed to that channel.

Community board

The name and message you post are stored. For private posts a salted hash of your password is stored, not the password.

Email and Telegram

If you write to us, we keep the conversation to answer you.

Third parties

The site is served by Cloudflare. Fonts are loaded from jsDelivr. Exchange and YouTube links take you to those services under their own policies.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: support@javidtrading.com.
