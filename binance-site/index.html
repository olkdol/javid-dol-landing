How I get paid

The bot is free. No subscription, no license key, no paid tier, no upsell.

I'm registered as a broker with the exchange. When the bot places an order, the exchange gives me a share of the trading fee you were already paying. Your fee is identical either way. Nothing is added to your cost.

Two things you should know before you decide anything:

  1. It only works for accounts opened after finding the bot. If you already have a Binance account, I earn nothing from your trading — ever. You're still welcome to use it. I'm telling you because you should know exactly where my incentive is and isn't.
  2. Please don't open a second account to change that. Binance prohibits it and it can get your account restricted. That's a bad trade for you and I don't want it.

Also: the sign-up link on this page is the plain Binance registration page, not a referral link. Check the URL. Attribution happens when the API key is created, so I lose nothing by not tracking you.

The conflict, stated plainly

I get paid per trade. That gives me a reason to want the bot to trade more. Read anything I say about trade frequency with that in mind. I can't remove the incentive — it's how this is funded. What I can do is show you the limits:

  • The settings that decide how often it trades live in a file on your computer. Not on my server. I can't change them, and I never see them.
  • Position size is 1/300 of your balance per entry. It is not built to churn.
  • Shadow mode exists so you can watch it before it touches money — the opposite of what I'd build if I wanted your trade count up.
  • I publish the record that makes me look worst: a position that ran to ROE -1,960%, and the day my own panel showed "100% win rate" while the account was carrying -768 USDT unrealized.

That position closed at +50.77%. Read it as one sample, not as evidence the approach works. The same path ends in liquidation often enough that you should assume it will happen to you at some point.

Futures trading can lose your entire balance through liquidation. Automation does not reduce that — it removes hesitation, which cuts both ways. Past results do not predict future ones. This is not investment advice.

FAQ

The Questions You'd Ask Before Paying

…except there's nothing to pay. It's free. Still, here's what you'll want to know:

Is it really free? No hidden costs?
Yes — no subscription, no purchase price, no performance fee. The only revenue source is Binance's official Broker Program: a share of the trading fee you already pay Binance gets rebated to us, by Binance.
Is it safe to enter my API key?
Your API key is encrypted and stored locally on your own PC — it is never transmitted anywhere, and withdrawal permission is never required.
Are profits guaranteed?
No. It's a tool that executes a defined strategy 24/7 without emotion. Crypto futures carry a serious risk of loss — validate in Shadow Mode before going live.
How close is Shadow Mode to real trading?
Shadow Mode uses the real exchange's live API with virtual USDT — signals, timing, and data are identical to live trading; only the real orders are withheld. This is not a testnet and not a generic paper-trading simulator — those run on separate sandbox data that can lag or diverge from the real market. Shadow Mode trades against the same live order book everyone else sees.
What happens if my PC shuts down mid-trade?
New entries and position management stop. Positions already open remain on the exchange, and the bot re-detects and resumes managing them on restart. An always-on mini PC or low-cost cloud VM is recommended.
What's the minimum capital?
We recommend 1,000 USDT or more so per-trade sizing (up to 1/300th of balance) stays above exchange minimum-notional limits.
What about flash crashes and liquidation risk?
Five layers of exit logic manage every position, but no system can fully eliminate the risk of violent moves in futures markets. Keep leverage conservative.
Can I use it without knowing how to code?
Yes — download, double-click JaviD_Future_Bot, paste your API key, pick coins, start. No code, anywhere. The User Manual covers it in 9 real screens.
Which exchanges and markets are supported?
Currently Binance USDⓈ-M Futures only, monitoring 500+ symbols simultaneously.
Does it run on Windows and macOS?
Yes — both. No high-end hardware needed, just a PC that stays on 24/7 with an internet connection.
Do I have to sign up through your referral link?
You can use an existing account, but only partner-link signups generate the fee rebate that keeps this software free.
How do I set up Telegram alerts?
Enter your bot token and chat ID in Settings — you'll get balance, P&L, ROI, win rate and open-position reports on request or on schedule.
What's the refund policy?
The price is $0, so there's nothing to refund. Delete it anytime — no cancellation flow, no penalty.
How do updates work?
New versions are announced on this page and in the Telegram channel. Download and overwrite — settings are preserved.
